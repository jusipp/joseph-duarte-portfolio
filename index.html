About

A fresh graduate who likes fixing things properly

I'm a fresh Information Technology graduate from Saint Joseph Institute of Technology, with a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology. I'm at the start of my career and looking for an entry-level role in IT support, technical support, help desk, or junior web development.
